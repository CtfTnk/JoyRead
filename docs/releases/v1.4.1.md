### 已知限制

- **书库数据库未加密：** 隐藏书籍的标题、标签、书单名、文件名和阅读进度仍可读取。隐藏之前产生的副本可能留在备份和快照中。详见[隐藏空间保护什么，不保护什么](https://github.com/CtfTnk/JoyRead/blob/main/docs/MANUAL.zh-CN.md#隐藏空间保护什么不保护什么)。

# JoyRead 1.4.1

No more stray windows. JoyRead 1.4.1 stops blank windows and Windows console windows from flashing up, keeps Hidden Space dialogs in place while they work, and explains how to replace an older copy on macOS.

## What's fixed

- **No more flashing blank windows:** when JoyRead rebuilt dialog buttons, the Filter dialog, tag lists, the sidebar's collections, Settings pages, the Reader's contents and bookmarks, or thumbnails in book details, an old control could briefly appear as a blank window of its own, most visibly on Windows.
- **No console window on Windows:** reading a 7z or RAR book no longer flashes a console window from the extraction helper.
- **Hidden Space dialogs stay in place:** while a passphrase is checked or a change is applied, the dialog greys out under a busy title instead of switching to a separate progress panel. After hiding or unhiding, the shelf refreshes in place instead of passing through its loading state.
- **Japanese and Chinese names in ZIP books:** ZIP/CBZ books made on Japanese or Chinese Windows often store folder and file names in Shift-JIS, GBK or Big5. Those names no longer show as garbled characters in the Contents, and pages sort by the real names. Books already in the Library show the corrected names the next time they open. A book that was repackaged at import, such as an archive nested inside another, or one sealed in Hidden Space, keeps its old names until it is imported again from the original file.
- **Updating on macOS:** the DMG window, the README, and the user manual now say to quit JoyRead first and choose **Replace** when Finder asks.

## Upgrade and compatibility

Upgrading from 1.4.0 adds no database migration; the library stays on schema 15. Your library, collections, tags, custom order, covers, reading progress, per-book settings, and Hidden Space are retained. Upgrading from 1.3.1 or earlier moves the library to schema 15, after which earlier versions cannot open it, so back up your library first; books hidden by an earlier version stay hidden but unencrypted until the first unlock. ZIP/CBZ, 7z/CB7, RAR/CBR, and PDF remain supported (except password-protected archives); EPUB remains disabled.

## Known limitations

- **The library database is not encrypted:** hidden books' titles, tags, collection names, file names, and reading progress stay readable. Copies made before hiding can survive in backups and snapshots. See [what Hidden Space protects](https://github.com/CtfTnk/JoyRead/blob/main/docs/MANUAL.md#what-hidden-space-protects-and-what-it-does-not).
- **Password-protected archives cannot be encrypted:** they stay hidden and are marked Not encrypted.
- **Logs contain book names:** logs record the file paths and names of books you import, open or export, including books you hide later. Check a log or crash report before sending it; Diagnostics can delete the logs.
- Very large progressive JPEG pages can still noticeably increase process memory while decoding.
