# JoyRead 1.0.2

**Your local library, easier to manage. Your next book, ready to read.**

## What's new

- **A more complete three-language experience.** New profiles follow the system language across macOS, Windows, and Ubuntu/Linux, choosing English, Simplified Chinese, or Japanese with English fallback. Existing explicit language choices survive upgrades.
- **Localized in-app names:** 欣阅 / JoyRead / ジョイヨミ. Installer names, application identity, and data paths stay JoyRead.
- **Live language switching** updates open windows, menus, and dialogs while preserving input, reading state, and background tasks. Translation fixes cover Hidden Space, recovery screens, tag operations, reader prompts, and tooltips. Chinese/Japanese Qt widget translations are bundled.
- **Archive and cache fixes** improve cache ownership, staging, and resource cleanup.
- **Safer library resets** wait for SQLite to release its files, fixing a shutdown/reset race on Windows.
- **Packaging improvements** cover macOS arm64, Windows x64, and Linux amd64/arm64, including Windows UTF-8 tests and Miniforge libffi compatibility.

## Read now, organise for later

Read **ZIP/CBZ, 7z/CB7, RAR/CBR, and PDF**. Drag a book onto **Read** to open it immediately or **Import** to keep a managed copy. JoyRead can also be selected through your OS's **Open With** integration. Switch between cards and lists, organise tags and collections, and keep selected books out of the ordinary shelf with Hidden Space.

In book details, double-click the **title, author, or language** to adjust it, or double-click the **cover** to edit it. Supported embedded **`meta.json` / `ComicInfo.xml`** metadata can populate the title, author, tags, and language during import.

## Known limitations

- **Encrypted comics are readable but cannot be imported.** Passwords are used for the current reading session only.
- **Hidden Space only hides content in the GUI; it does not encrypt files.**
- Decrypted page caches are plaintext. **Delete cached pages when closing** is enabled by default. For 7z, RAR, and legacy ZipCrypto ZIP, helper command-line passwords may be briefly visible to same-user processes. AES ZIP is decrypted in-process.
- EPUB / light novel reading remains disabled. Solid-RAR performance remains unverified.
- Native desktop validation for this version remains pending: fresh installation, upgrade/uninstall, OS file activation, language switching across multiple windows, native dialogs, and Ubuntu Wayland/X11 behavior.
