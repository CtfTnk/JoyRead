# JoyRead 1.3.1

A clearer reading interface and smoother page preparation. JoyRead 1.3.1 refines Detail and navigation panels, progress indicators, and multi-book drag previews, and improves background loading for random-access archives.

## What's improved

- **Detail and Topic panels:** fixed title/close rows, refined toolbar spacing, and more room for Detail content. Close buttons keep a borderless resting state with clearer hover and pressed feedback.
- **Balanced translucent surfaces:** refined backgrounds and thin borders for panels, menu items, action buttons, and selection states. The Topic selector retains its white background, distinct from gray switches.
- **Clearer reading progress:** matching read/unread colors across the Reader, book cards, and Detail. The Reader handle is now 22 × 10 px; top and bottom controls use uniform translucent backgrounds with subtle separators.
- **Thumbnail selection:** images and page numbers share one selection area, with aligned top/left/right highlight insets and more consistent hover and selected states.
- **Multi-book custom sorting:** compact cover/title cards form a fan while dragging, with a selection count and a stable pointer offset.
- **Background loading:** confirmed random-access, non-solid sources use bounded concurrent page and thumbnail preparation, favoring visible content. Rapid navigation and closing retire obsolete requests so stale results cannot reappear. Solid archives, unknown-access sources, and PDFs retain their existing serial preparation policy.
- **Documentation:** complete Chinese and Japanese manuals, with clearer tag-creation instructions and platform-specific storage paths.
- **Windows text layout:** elided labels retain a full font line box when baseline spacing is shorter, preventing a one-pixel shortfall without enlarging panels or controls.
- **Image-format compatibility:** when an older Qt build cannot load a native codec, existing Pillow codecs decode and pre-scale in workers, restoring Ubuntu ARM64 WebP reading, thumbnails, and cover previews.

## Upgrade and compatibility

Upgrading from 1.3.0 adds no database migration. Your library, collections, tags, custom order, covers, reading progress, and existing per-book settings are retained. Back up your library before upgrading. Preloading still defaults to 4 previous / 8 following pages. Windows background mode and automatic idle memory cleanup remain enabled by default. ZIP/CBZ, 7z/CB7, RAR/CBR, and PDF remain supported; EPUB remains disabled.
